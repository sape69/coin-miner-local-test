Stelluriini Account Deletion

Request deletion of your Stelluriini account and associated account data.

How to delete your account

If you are signed in to the Stelluriini app, you can permanently delete your account directly from the app:

  • Open the Stelluriini menu.
  • Select Delete Account.
  • Confirm the deletion.

The app sends the authenticated account deletion request to Stelluriini's secure backend.

Important:

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Account data associated with the Stelluriini account is deleted as part of the account deletion process.

Cannot access the app?

If you cannot access the Stelluriini app, you can request account deletion by contacting the Stelluriini team.

Account deletion requests:

stelluriini.app@gmail.com

Please send the request from the email address associated with your Stelluriini account.
