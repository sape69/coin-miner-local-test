Stelluriini Account Deletion

Request deletion of your Stelluriini account and associated account data.

Delete your account in the app

If you can access the Stelluriini app, you can permanently delete your account directly from the application.

  1. Open the Stelluriini app and sign in.
  2. Open the menu and select Delete Account.
  3. Confirm that you want to permanently delete your account.

Cannot access the app?

If you cannot access your Stelluriini account, you can request account deletion by contacting us using the email address below.

Account deletion request stelluriini.app@gmail.com
Important: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. When an account deletion request is processed, the associated Stelluriini account data is deleted according to the application's data deletion process.
