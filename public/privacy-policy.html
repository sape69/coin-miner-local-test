🐱 Stelluriini Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Stelluriini handles information when you use the Stelluriini mobile application.

1. About Stelluriini

Stelluriini is a digital mining-themed application inspired by Stella the cat. The application provides account-based features, mining status information, hash rate information, STL-related information, Power Boost functionality and other application features.

Stelluriini uses cloud services to provide authentication, application functionality and server-side data processing.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the application and which third-party services are active, Stelluriini may process the following categories of information.

Account information

  • Email address used for authentication.
  • Authentication information required to maintain your account.
  • User account identifiers associated with your Stelluriini account.
  • Username or profile information provided through the application.

Application and account data

  • Mining status and mining-related information.
  • Hash rate and mining progress information.
  • STL-related balance and transaction information maintained by the application.
  • Information required to provide application features and prevent misuse.

Advertising, device and diagnostic information

Stelluriini uses Google Mobile Ads / AdMob for rewarded advertising. Google and its advertising services may process information associated with advertising and the device on which the application is used.

Depending on the device, operating system, advertising settings, consent choices and region, this may include:

  • IP address.
  • General or approximate location that may be inferred from an IP address.
  • Advertising identifiers and other device or app identifiers.
  • Information about the device and operating environment.
  • Application interaction information, such as app launches, taps and interactions with advertisements.
  • Information about rewarded advertisement views and related ad interactions.
  • Diagnostic and technical information used to maintain reliability, security and performance.

The exact information processed by Google Mobile Ads / AdMob can vary depending on the user's device, operating system, advertising settings, consent status, region and the advertising services available to the user.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used for the following purposes:

  • Creating and managing your Stelluriini account.
  • Authenticating users and providing secure sign-in functionality.
  • Providing mining and application functionality.
  • Maintaining mining status, hash rate and related account information.
  • Providing Power Boost and rewarded-ad functionality.
  • Displaying and measuring advertisements.
  • Understanding application interactions and improving application functionality.
  • Diagnosing technical problems and improving application reliability.
  • Protecting the application against abuse, fraud and unauthorized activity.
  • Maintaining the security of the application and its services.
  • Responding to support, privacy and account deletion requests.

4. Third-Party Services

Stelluriini uses third-party services to provide parts of the application.

Firebase

Stelluriini uses Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Firebase Cloud Functions. These services may process information required for authentication, account management, server-side processing and application functionality.

Google Mobile Ads / AdMob

Stelluriini uses Google Mobile Ads / AdMob to display rewarded advertisements. Ad-related information may be processed by Google according to Google's applicable privacy policies, advertising technologies and consent requirements.

AdMob may process information such as IP address, approximate location derived from IP address, device or advertising identifiers, application interactions, advertisement interactions and diagnostic information.

Stelluriini uses Google's consent mechanisms where applicable. Depending on the user's region and consent status, available advertising and personalization features may differ.

Third-party service providers process information according to their own applicable policies and terms.

5. Sharing of Information

Stelluriini does not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed or shared with service providers that are necessary to operate the application, including authentication, cloud infrastructure, database services, server-side functions and advertising services.

Google Mobile Ads / AdMob may receive or process advertising, device, identifier, interaction, diagnostic and approximate location information as necessary to provide advertising and related services.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law, legal process, security requirements or to protect the rights, safety and integrity of the application and its users.

6. Data Security

Stelluriini uses commercially available cloud infrastructure and security mechanisms provided by its service providers to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration or disclosure.

Authentication and application data are processed through the services used by the application, including Firebase.

Data transmitted between the application and applicable services is protected using encryption in transit, including secure transport mechanisms such as HTTPS/TLS where supported by the service.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should therefore also protect their account credentials and device.

7. Data Retention

Account and application information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide the requested application services, maintain security, comply with applicable legal obligations and resolve legitimate disputes.

Advertising and diagnostic information processed by third-party services may be retained according to the applicable policies and retention practices of those services.

When an account deletion request is completed, associated account information will be deleted except where information must be retained for legitimate legal, security, fraud-prevention or regulatory reasons.

8. Account Deletion

Users can request deletion of their Stelluriini account and associated account data.

To request account deletion, contact us using the email address below and clearly state that you want your Stelluriini account and associated data deleted.

Account deletion email: Stelluriini.app@gmail.com

We will process account deletion requests within a reasonable period and will delete associated account data unless certain information must be retained for legitimate legal, security, fraud-prevention or regulatory reasons.

9. Children's Privacy

Stelluriini is not specifically directed toward children. Users should only create an account and use the application when permitted to do so under the applicable laws of their country or region.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information without appropriate authorization, please contact us so that the information can be reviewed and appropriate action can be taken.

10. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The Stelluriini mobile application does not use traditional website cookies as its primary application storage mechanism.

Third-party services used by the application may use identifiers, storage technologies or similar technologies as necessary for their services, including advertising, measurement, analytics and security functionality.
